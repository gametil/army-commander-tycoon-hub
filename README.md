## ✨ Features

### 🏗️ Auto Tycoon & Upgrades
- **Auto Upgrade Buildings**: Automatically purchases building upgrades as soon as cash is available.
- **Priority Modes**:
  - `Cheapest First` *(Fastest compounding income)*
  - `Income First` *(Oil Rigs, Refinery, Generator, Handpump)*
  - `HQ First` *(Rushes Headquarters for tier progression)*
  - `All In Order`
- **Instant Mass Upgrade**: Single-click button to batch purchase all affordable upgrades.
- **Daily Rewards**: Auto claim daily crate cash rewards.

### 🛢️ AFK Auto Pump Oil (Unlimited Cash Farm)
- Automatically teleports you onto the handpump stand.
- Pumps oil continuously at optimal stroke speed (~0.48s).
- **Position Lock**: Keeps your character locked on the pump stand so you don't get pushed by tanks or jeeps.
- **Anti-AFK Protection**: Bypasses the Roblox 20-minute idle kick.
- Generates over **$100,000+ extra cash per minute** to feed directly into base upgrades.

### ⚔️ Army & Combat Orders
- **Map Conquest**: Dispatches Infantry, Vehicles, Air, and Naval units to capture any of the 17 strategic points across the map.
- **Quick Tactical Actions**: One-click deployment to Central Airfield, Willow Village, Radar Hill, and Oil Refinery.
- **Auto Conquest**: Background task that repeatedly sends available forces to capture enemy points.

### 🔥 Rebirth Automation
- Real-time rebirth dashboard (Rank, Permanent Bonus %, Cost).
- Auto Rebirth option when HQ and cash requirements are satisfied.

### 🏃 Player Mods & Teleports
- Custom WalkSpeed (16 - 120) & JumpPower (50 - 250).
- Infinite Jump & Tactical Night Vision.
- Fast travel teleports to Base, Handpump, HQ, and all 17 strategic map capture points.

### 🎁 Settings & Promo Codes
- Built-in promo code redeemer.
- Clean unload option.
